ELIAS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who uses ELIAS

ELIAS is a personal assistant used only by its creator, Nicholas. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's data is collected.

What ELIAS accesses through Google

  • Gmail: to send draft emails to its owner for approval, and to read the owner's replies (APPROVE or REJECT) to those drafts.
  • Google Sheets: to read and update the owner's own content-planning spreadsheet.

Where the data goes

ELIAS and its automations run on the owner's own computer. Data from Google is used only to perform the tasks above. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API data

ELIAS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page.
